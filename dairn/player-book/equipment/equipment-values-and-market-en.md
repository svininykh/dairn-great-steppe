# Equipment, Valuables, and the Marketplace

*Structural basis: Cairn 2e — Marketplace.*

## Inventory and Carried Items

An ordinary item occupies **1 Inventory Slot**, a **Bulky** item **2**, and a **Petty** item **0**. A character's total limit is **10 slots**. A **Bag** has a capacity of up to **6 slots**, included in the character's total of **10**; it does not increase the total Inventory limit. If all 10 slots are occupied, the character's Hit Protection becomes 0. Full rules are given in [The Character in Play](../character/character-in-play.md).

During character creation, items are determined using the [starting Inventory table, `7d6`](../../tables/starting-inventory-7d6-en.md). Their details are collected in the separate [basic equipment table](../../tables/basic-equipment-en.md).

## Weapons

The [reference table](../../tables/basic-equipment-en.md#weapons) gives each weapon's damage die and the number of Inventory Slots it occupies. Record the damage die beside the weapon's name on your character sheet.

A shortbow and quiver of arrows count as a single Bulky set. The axe in the basic set is an ordinary axe and occupies one slot; the sabre corresponds to a sword.

Attack procedures, Enhanced and Impaired Damage, attacking with two weapons, and ammunition are covered in [Combat](../danger-and-combat/combat.md).

## Armor

The [reference](../../tables/basic-equipment-en.md#armor) lists all six
types: shield, helmet, gambeson, brigandine, chainmail, and mirror armor.

Subtract Armor from Damage before applying it to Hit Protection.
`+1` adds to applicable protection and provides 1 even without other armor.
A shield protects while held and used; wearable equipment protects while
worn. Combined use must be possible in the situation: a hand holding a
shield cannot simultaneously help draw a bow with both hands.
Total Armor cannot exceed 3. For example, chainmail and a shield provide
Armor 3. Worn equipment still occupies Inventory Slots; it does not
increase Hit Protection.

[Brigandine](../../tables/basic-equipment-en.md#armor) provides Armor 1,
occupies 2 Inventory Slots, and is Bulky. It protects only while worn.
Total Armor cannot exceed 3.

The complete [Mirror armor](../../tables/basic-equipment-en.md#mirror-armor)
set provides Armor 3, occupies 2 slots, and is Bulky. The armor worn
underneath is included in these statistics.

## Ordinary Equipment

Basic Travel Gear and Tools, including the number of Inventory Slots each item occupies, are listed in the [reference table](../../tables/basic-equipment-en.md). A felt rug occupies 2 slots; a needle with strong thread and a whetstone occupy 0 slots; the other listed items and sets occupy 1 slot each.

What an item can do depends on its purpose and the circumstances. If its use involves risk or uncertainty, apply [Game Procedures](../playing-in-the-world/game-procedures.md).

## Provisions

Starting Food — zhaya, toqash, qurt, talqan, or a listed combination — lasts **3 days** and occupies **1 Inventory Slot**. The specific selection is determined by the [starting Supplies table](../../tables/starting-inventory-7d6-en.md#supplies-water-food-and-fire).

## Supplies

**Water, Food, and Fire** are determined separately during creation. Starting Water for 1 day occupies 0 slots; for 2 days, 1 slot; for 3 days, 2 slots and is Bulky.

The contents of the Fire supply, how long it lasts, and the slots it occupies depend on the roll result and are listed in the [starting Supplies table](../../tables/starting-inventory-7d6-en.md#supplies-water-food-and-fire). Record the specific supplies and the number of Inventory Slots they occupy. The consequences of lacking food, water, or proper rest are described in [Game Procedures](../playing-in-the-world/game-procedures.md#изнурён).
