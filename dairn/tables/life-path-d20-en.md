# DAIRN — Life Path d20

A **Life Path** describes your character's past experience, not a class or current profession. Choose a suitable Life Path or roll **d20**.

| d20 | Life Path | Experience | What the Experience Covers |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Raised in the Saddle** | Riding | Handling horses, living and travelling on horseback |
| 2 | **Raised Among Clan Warriors** | Warrior customs | Weapons, training, and the daily life of warriors |
| 3 | **Hunted with Birds of Prey** | Falconry | Hunting birds, tracking, and taking game |
| 4 | **Tended Herds** | Herding | Herds, animal behaviour, grazing, and steppe life |
| 5 | **Followed the Trail** | Tracking | Finding and reading tracks, pursuit, and determining the direction of travel |
| 6 | **Gathered Healing Herbs** | Steppe plants | Finding, gathering, and using plants for healing |
| 7 | **Trained in Healing** | Healing | Treating wounds and injuries, caring for the sick and injured |
| 8 | **Learned a Master's Craft** | A chosen craft | Materials, tools, making and repairing things |
| 9 | **Raised at the Forge** | Smithing | Metal, smithing tools, making, appraising, and repairing metal objects |
| 10 | **Travelled with Caravans** | Caravan work | Long journeys, goods, stopping places, and hazards along the way |
| 11 | **Knows the Steppe Roads** | Steppe routes | Routes, landmarks, fords, campsites, and wayfinding |
| 12 | **Guarded Camps** | Camp guarding | Keeping watch, night guard duty, recognising danger, and defending a camp |
| 13 | **Kept Lore in Song** | Oral traditions | Songs, legends, oral history, and preserving knowledge |
| 14 | **Learned to Read and Write** | Reading and writing | Reading, writing, records, and the written tradition |
| 15 | **Carried News Between People** | Messenger work | Delivering messages, finding people, roads, and the customs of different communities |
| 16 | **Traded and Bartered Goods** | Trading | Bargaining, barter, appraising goods, and understanding their worth |
| 17 | **Hiding Their Past** | Choose an area of Experience | The player also determines the reason for concealment |
| 18 | **Foundling** | Life among strangers | Unwritten rules, others' attitudes, and finding a place among new people |
| 19 | **Keeper of Clan Memory** | Clan memory | Genealogies, kinship, old obligations, and past events |
| 20 | **Witnessed the Impossible** | Foresight | Signs of the near future; governed by a special rule |

Experience and the unique element for each result are listed together in the [Life Path Experience and unique elements table](life-path-unique-elements-d20-en.md).

## Notes

- Experience is not a skill system, has no numerical value, and grants no bonuses to Checks.
- For No. 8, the player chooses one craft; smithing is the separate result No. 9.
- No. 14, **Learned to Read and Write**, is not a prerequisite for using a **Book** or a **Word**.
- For No. 17, the player determines one main area of Experience.
- No. 18, the **Foundling**, uses the special rule for an additional secret Omen.
- For No. 20, the full Foresight rule is given in the linked table.
