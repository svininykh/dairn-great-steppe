# DAIRN — Basic Equipment

A reference for Weapons, Travel Gear, and Tools used in [starting Inventory, `7d6`](starting-inventory-7d6-en.md). Rules for tracking items are given in [Equipment, Valuables, and the Marketplace](../player-book/equipment/equipment-values-and-market-en.md).

## Weapons

| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Shortbow + quiver of arrows | d6 | 2 | Single Bulky set |
| Spear | d8 | 1 | — |
| Axe | d8 | 1 | Ordinary axe, not a Bulky weapon |
| Dagger | d6 | 1 | — |
| Cudgel | d6 | 1 | — |
| Sabre | d8 | 1 | — |
| Qamshy (whip) | d4 | 1 | No additional combat properties |

The composite bow and quiver in the pilot's initial character profiles use
the Shortbow + quiver of arrows definition: `d6`, 2 slots, one Bulky set.
Composite construction grants no separate bonus.

The qamshy was added for Yersin's visible whip in the pilot (Issue #25).
This is an authored DAIRN game definition: an ordinary one-slot item with
base damage `d4`, matching an unarmed Attack in the Combat rules. The weapon
itself does not make an Attack Impaired. Damage, range, Blast, disarming,
and other special properties are not inferred from the image.

A weapon's damage die is a property of the weapon. Record it alongside the item's name; no separate roll is needed to determine it during character creation. When Attacking, use the [Combat rules](../player-book/danger-and-combat/combat.md).

## Armor

| Item | Armor | Inventory Slots | Note |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Shield | +1 | 1 | Protects while held and used |
| Helmet | +1 | 1 | Protects while worn |
| Gambeson | +1 | 1 | Protective clothing made of quilted layers |
| Brigandine | 1 | 2 | Bulky |
| Chainmail | 2 | 2 | Bulky |
| Mirror armor | 3 | 2 | Bulky; complete set |

**Reading Armor values.** A number without `+` gives the armor
value of the item itself: brigandine 1, chainmail 2, mirror armor 3.
A `+1` adds to other applicable Armor. Without other armor, that item
provides Armor 1 on its own; without protective equipment, Armor is 0.
When using just one item, `1` and `+1` give the same result.

Examples: a shield alone gives Armor 1; chainmail and a shield give
Armor 3; chainmail, a shield, and a helmet still give Armor 3 because
that is the overall limit. Items must be worn or used, and their combined
use must be possible in the situation. These values reduce Damage;
they do not add to Hit Protection (HP).

Brigandine is armor made of metal plates secured to a cloth or leather
backing. It provides Armor 1 while worn; carrying it in Inventory
does not provide protection.

DAIRN uses the names **Берен сауыт / Куяк / Brigandine**. This is a
mapping for game adaptation, not a claim that the historical terms
are fully equivalent. Its statistics come from
[Cairn 2e — Marketplace, Armor](https://cairnrpg.com/second-edition/players-guide/marketplace/#armor)
by Yochai Gal: Brigandine, Armor 1, Bulky.
A protective equipment roll of 4 grants brigandine.

### Shield, helmet, gambeson, and chainmail

**Қалқан / Щит / Shield** is handheld protection.
**Дулыға / Шлем / Helmet** is protective headgear.
**Қаттама сауыт / Стёганый доспех / Gambeson** is protective clothing
made of quilted fabric layers with padding; an ordinary travelling
cloak does not have this property.
**Кіреуке / Кольчуга / Chainmail** is armor made of interlinked
metal rings.

Their Armor values and Inventory requirements come from
[Cairn 2e — Marketplace, Armor](https://cairnrpg.com/second-edition/players-guide/marketplace/#armor)
by Yochai Gal. The names are adapted for DAIRN.
All six protective equipment entries are available in this reference;
the starting Inventory `7d6` table grants one of them with a separate die.

### Mirror armor

**Шарайна / Зерцало / Mirror armor** are the names of the same equipment
entry in DAIRN's language editions.

Historical mirror armor uses large steel plates, slightly curved to
fit the body and joined with straps and buckles. In Kazakh tradition,
sharaina was generally worn over another suit of armor as additional
protection. This description follows
[K. S. Akhmetzhan's study of Kazakh armor](https://e-history.kz/ru/news/show/5795).
It is distinct from a complete European suit of plate armor.

In the equipment table, **Mirror armor** means a complete armor set
with mirror plates, including the armor worn underneath. The entire
set provides **Armor 3**, occupies **2 Inventory Slots**, and is
**Bulky**. These statistics apply while the set is worn. The underlying
armor is already included: do not add its Armor or slots again.
An individual plate does not receive the statistics of the whole set.

This adapts the **Plate** entry from
[Cairn 2e — Marketplace, Armor](https://cairnrpg.com/second-edition/players-guide/marketplace/#armor)
by Yochai Gal. The game mapping does not imply historical equivalence
of construction or protective performance. A protective equipment roll of 6 grants mirror armor.

## Basis of Adaptation

Weapon damage dice are based on [Cairn 2e — Marketplace, Weapons](https://cairnrpg.com/second-edition/players-guide/marketplace/#weapons) by Yochai Gal: bows, daggers, and cudgels use `d6`; spears, axes, and swords use `d8`. In DAIRN, the axe corresponds to an ordinary axe, and the sabre to a sword. The number of Inventory Slots each item occupies is unchanged from the current DAIRN starting Inventory table.
